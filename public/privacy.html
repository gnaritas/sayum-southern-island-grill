Privacy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is the whole of it.

What we collect

The contact form sends your name — and optionally your phone and email — plus your note straight to the restaurant’s inbox. It is used only to answer you. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, and there are no cookies, no advertising trackers, and no third-party scripts on this site. The map embed on the contact page may set its own cookies once you interact with it — Google’s privacy policy covers that part.

Who runs this site

SaYum Southern Island Grill operates this site for its guests. Want your note deleted after we’ve answered it? Say so in the form — one sentence is enough.
