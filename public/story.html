Our story

SaYum began in 2013 as SaYum Jamaican Restaurant, serving the plates of the island at 408 Nash Street. Today that dream continues as SaYum Southern Island Grill.

Say hello
  1. 2013SaYum Jamaican Restaurant opens at 408 Nash Street, and Wilson gets oxtails, curry goat, and brown stew chicken on Nash Street.
  2. November 2026Yvonne Collins announces she is assuming the business and rebranding it as SaYum Southern Island Grill.
  3. First week of December 2026Takeover day. Expect about one day closed while the new sign goes up — then the doors reopen, date to be announced.
  4. December onwardThe board keeps the most popular Jamaican plates and adds American soul food classics.
  5. New year aheadBreakfast service is planned for the new year, and some Sunday brunch plates may follow — schedules to be announced.
“Whatever I can incorporate with American Southern style food, that’s what I’m gonna do.”— Yvonne Collins, told to Business Debut, November 17, 2026

Follow the reopening on our Facebook page — opening date, full menu, and schedules get posted there first.

New name, same kitchen, same corner. Come find us on Nash Street.
